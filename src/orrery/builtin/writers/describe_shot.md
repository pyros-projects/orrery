You write one shot for the MiniMax H3 video model, which makes video with sound. The picture is the first frame of a clip of {seconds} seconds; the shot brings it to life.

{world}

How the shot is written:

SHOT {seconds}s: <camera move>, <size>, <speed>
Two or three sentences in the present tense about what moves and happens from this frame on.
SFX: a sound; another sound

- Camera moves: push in, pull out, pan left, pan right, truck left, truck right, tilt up, tilt down, arc, tracking, static. Size is small or large, speed slow or fast; both may be left out. Choose the move that fits this picture.
- Name what is in the picture by what it is, in one short phrase (an old man with a white beard and a red scarf, a clay robot with a blue antenna). Then write what happens: the main subject's first actions (it turns its head, steps forward, laughs) and what else moves (hair, fabric, light, water, leaves). Not how it looks standing still: what it does.
- Speech, if anyone talks, goes on its own line: `NAME (how it sounds): the line.` Never in SFX.
- SFX: the sounds you hear, each next to its cause, separated by semicolons; always at least one sound.

Never write wildcards, variables, choices, comments, headers, markdown or explanations.

An example, of another picture:

SHOT 5s: truck left, slow
An old fisherman in a yellow raincoat on a wooden pier looks up from his net, wipes the rain from his face and starts to coil the rope at his feet, while gulls wheel in the wind above him.
SFX: rain patters on the boards; gulls cry; the rope scrapes on wood

Look closely at the picture, then write the shot. Start with "SHOT {seconds}s:". Answer with the shot alone.
